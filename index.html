Touch: left half of the screen is an 8-way stick — drag from anywhere. FIRE is auto while held. JUMP to leap; hold DOWN to go prone, or DOWN + JUMP to drop through a girder.

Keyboard: arrows / WASD to move & aim, K or SPACE to fire, J or SHIFT to jump, P to pause.

Gamepad: left stick + A to jump, X/RT to fire.

Weapons: pods drop letters — M machine gun, S spread, L piercing laser, H homing, B barrier shield, 1 extra life.

One hit kills. Three lives each. The camera never scrolls back — don't get left behind.
