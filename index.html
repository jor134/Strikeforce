Controls

Phone: touch anywhere on the left half to raise the stick — it moves and aims at the same time, Contra-style 8-way. Hold down + no sideways input to crouch. Right side: FIRE and JUMP. Auto-fire is on by default; toggle it above the jump button.

Keyboard: A/D move · W/S aim up & crouch · K or Space jump · J or Z fire.

Gamepad: left stick aims and moves · A jump · X or right trigger fire.

Jump straight down through a thin platform by holding down as you jump. Falling into a pit costs a life. Pick up letters to swap weapons: S spread · M machine gun · L piercing laser · H repairs armour.
